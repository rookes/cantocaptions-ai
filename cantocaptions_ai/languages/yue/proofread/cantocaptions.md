# CantoCaptions conventions

Where your general knowledge of written Cantonese disagrees with these, these win.

## Sentence-final particles

Each particle character stands for exactly one syllable and tone, so the character must
match what the speaker said. Where a note is given, it says what the particle does, which
is how to tell from context which one was said. English glosses show meaning only.

**aa**
- 吖 aa1 — soft offering, suggestion or mild insistence; also passive-aggressive exasperation. `你講吖` "go ahead"; `你呢隻嘢吖` "you jerk!"
- 嗄 aa2 — contraction of 啊 + 吓.
- 啊 aa3 — general softener and warmth on a statement or question; giving an opinion or feeling. Usually tone only.
- 呀 aa4 — turns a plain statement into a question ("you're here?"); used when addressing someone; rarely, disgruntlement.
- 咓 aa5 — contraction of 𠻺 + 嗎.
- 𠻺 aa6 — a short break in speech; tone only, unless part of 𠻺嗎.
- 𡅅 aak3 — as 啊, with a sense of certainty or finality.

**baa, bo**
- 罷 baa2
- 噃 bo3

**gaa, gaak**
- 𠺢 gaa1 — mostly in 𠺢嘛 (see compounds).
- 𠿪 gaa2 — contraction of 㗎 + 吓.
- 㗎 gaa3 — assertion or emphasis, matter-of-fact tone; a statement contrary to what was assumed; or simply 嘅 + 啊. `佢唔會噉講㗎` "he wouldn't say that"
- 嘎 gaa4 — 㗎 as a question.
- 㗇 gaa5 — contraction of 噶 + 嗎.
- 噶 gaa6
- 𠺝 gaak3 — as 㗎, with certainty or finality.

**ge**
- 𠸏 ge2 — surprise, realisation, a noncommittal or uncertain tone (avoiding assertion), mild challenge. The CantoCaptions replacement for 嘅 in this role. `係𠸏` "uh-huh / I guess"; `唔見咗𠸏？` "it's gone?"
- 嘅 ge3 — possessive or nominalising (X 嘅 Y = "X's Y"), and flat assertion. Not the surprise particle; that is 𠸏.

**gwaa, haa, he, ho**
- 啩 gwaa3 — strongly held speculation: "probably", "I think".
- 吓 haa2 — checking, seeking affirmation ("…okay?"); alone, "huh?".
- 下 haa6
- 嚱 he2
- 嗬 ho2 — re-confirmation like 吓, but more unsure and questioning.

**laa, laak**
- 啦 laa1 — suggestion, urging, acceptance of a shared understanding, or obviousness; sometimes to list things. `走啦` "let's go"; `梗係唔係啦` "of course not!"
- 𠸎 laa2
- 喇 laa3 — change of state or newly relevant information; a goodbye; a stern command. `搞掂喇` "all done"; `走喇` "leave!"; `我走先喇` "I have to go"
- 嗱 laa4 — 喇 as a question (`你返嚟嗱？` "you're back?"); alone or line-initially, "here" / "now, …" / "look". `嗱，你嘅錢` "here's your money"
- 嚹 laa6
- 嘞 laak3 — as 喇, with certainty or finality.

**le, lo, lok, lu**
- 呢 le1 — softens a question, or "what about…?"; a filler to parse out ideas; rarely, line-initial incredulous "huh?". `你呢？` "what about you?"
- 咧 le4
- 哩 le5 — softens or seeks agreement: "…right?"
- 囖 lo1 — obviousness or resignation ("well, there you go"); a straightforward recommendation; "just", especially with 咪. `咪係囖` "exactly"; `你咪去囖` "just go then"
- 咯 lo3 — proposes an action, often with 不如; rarely, in place of 喇.
- 囉 lo4 — as 咯.
- 嚛 lok6 — reporting what was already said, slightly negative ("you should have known"). `我已經講過嚛` "I've already said so"
- 嚕 lu3 — gleeful, happy 喇.

**maa, me**
- 嘛 maa3 — in written-style questions; in compounds like 吖嘛, shared understanding ("…, you know?").
- 嗎 maa5
- 咩 me1 — a question tinged with doubt or surprise. `係咩？` "really?"

**tim, waa, wo**
- 𠻹 tim1 — "in addition / even / too".
- 哇 waa5 — disbelief or outrage (generally older speakers); sometimes a lazy 嗎 in compounds like 𠻺嗎.
- 喎 wo3 — emotional intensifier: shock, happiness, contempt ("oh…", "it's happening!").
- 啝 wo4 — 喎 as a question; also non-question surprise.
- 𡁜 wo5 — reported or second-hand information, or "note this": "apparently", "I hear", "you know". Not the same as 喎.

**zaa, ze, zek**
- 吒 zaa1 — mostly in 吒嘛 (see compounds).
- 咋 zaa3 — "only / merely".
- 喳 zaa4 — 咋 as a question.
- 𠾵 zaa5
- 咤 zaa6
- 啫 ze1 — "only / just", diminishing.
- 唧 zek1 — very pointed questions, or child-like glee. `好彩唧` "so lucky!"; `你做咩唧？` "what are you doing?"
